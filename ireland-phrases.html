Irish Gaelic Phrases

Sláinte SLAWN-cha "Cheers!" / "Good health!" (the classic toast)
Dia dhuit DEE-ah gwith Hello (literally "God be with you")
Fáilte FAHL-cheh Welcome
Céad Míle Fáilte KAYD MEE-luh FAHL-cheh "A Hundred Thousand Welcomes" (you'll see this everywhere)
Go raibh maith agat guh rev MAH ah-gut Thank you
Important: "Top of the morning to you" is NOT actually used by Irish people — it's a Hollywood stereotype and will get you gentle eye-rolls. Stick with "What's the craic?" and you'll fit right in.
