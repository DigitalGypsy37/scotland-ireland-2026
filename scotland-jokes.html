Scottish Jokes

General Scotland

What’s the difference between a bagpipe and an onion? Nobody cries when you chop up a bagpipe.
Why do bagpipers walk while they play? To get away from the sound.
Did you hear about the Scottish magician? He turned whisky into regret.
I came, I saw, I kilt.
What’s Scottish sunshine? A rumour!
40 degrees — Californians shiver uncontrollably. People in Scotland sunbathe.
A lady at the Highland Games asked a Scotsman: "Excuse me, do you mind telling me what’s worn under your kilt?" He replied: "Nothing is worn, madam. Everything is in perfect working order."
Why did the haggis refuse to go to the Burns Night dinner? It didn’t have the stomach for it.
A Scotsman, an Englishman, and an Australian walk into a bar. A fly lands in each pint. The Englishman pushes his away in disgust. The Australian fishes the fly out and keeps drinking. The Scotsman picks the fly up by the wings, holds it over the glass, and growls: "Spit it oot, ye wee devil!"
My dad used to say to me: "Pints, gallons, litres." Which I think speaks volumes. — Olaf Falafel, Edinburgh Fringe
They say whisky makes you live longer. I’m not immortal yet, but I’m giving it my best shot.
In Scotland, we have mixed feelings about global warming. We’ll all get to sit on the mountains and watch the English drown. — Frankie Boyle
What is the difference between a Scottish sheep farmer and a Rolling Stones song? One says "Hey you, get off of my cloud!" The other says "Hey McCloud, get off of my ewe!"
Rain is just sky whisky!

Edinburgh

Edinburgh and Glasgow — same country, two very different cities. When a gun goes off in Edinburgh, it’s one o’clock. — Kevin Bridges
Enjoying a reign-y day at Edinburgh Castle. Joust do it.
Why don’t secrets stay hidden in Edinburgh Castle? Too many tour guides — and at least three ghosts who can’t keep their mouths shut.
The One O’Clock Gun at Edinburgh Castle fires every day at 1pm. A tourist asked why they don’t fire it at noon. A local replied: "What? And waste eleven extra rounds?"
What did the ghost of Greyfriars Bobby say? "This graveyard is ruff around the edges, but I love it!"
Edinburgh’s Royal Mile is exactly one mile long. Unless you stop at every shop that sells tartan, in which case it takes three hours.
Edinburgh ghost tours claim there are over 500 ghosts in the city. The other 500,000 residents just look that way before their morning coffee.
Palace of Holyroodhouse is where the King stays when he’s in Edinburgh. The rest of the time, the ghosts of Mary Queen of Scots and David Rizzio have the place to themselves. Rizzio was murdered there in 1566 and they say you can still hear him complaining about the hospitality.
A man lost in Edinburgh asks a policeman: "Excuse me, is there a B&Q in Leith?" The policeman replies: "No sir, but there are two Ds and two Es in Dundee."
I couldn’t have afforded to live in a castle, but it would have been peasant.

Edinburgh Fringe One-Liners

Edinburgh hosts the world’s largest comedy festival every August. Here are some award-winning zingers to drop on the bus:

I was going to sail around the globe in the world’s smallest ship, but I bottled it. — Mark Simmons, 2024 Fringe winner
I started dating a zookeeper, but it turned out he was a cheetah. — Lorna Rose Treen, 2023 Fringe winner
I sailed through my driving test. That’s why I failed it. — Arthur Smith, Edinburgh Fringe
I had to visit the trauma unit last weekend. He prefers the term "dad." — Andy Gleeks, Edinburgh Fringe
I ate horse at a restaurant once — wasn’t great. Starter was all right but the mane was dreadful. — Alex Kitson, Edinburgh Fringe
I’ve been taking salsa lessons for months but I don’t feel like I’m progressing. It’s just one step forward… two steps back. — Alec Snook, Edinburgh Fringe
Edinburgh Fringe tip: Every August, the Edinburgh Festival Fringe hosts thousands of comedy shows — the world’s largest arts festival. Many of these jokes come from Fringe award winners. If you’re ever back in August, go!

Stirling & the Trossachs

What’s the difference between Stirling Castle and Mel Gibson? One actually looks like a legend.
William Wallace’s real sword is 5 feet 4 inches long. Mel Gibson is 5 feet 9. The sword nearly played the lead role instead.
Doune Castle doubled as Castle Leoch in Outlander and the French castle in Monty Python and the Holy Grail. It’s the most famous Scottish castle that nobody knows by its real name.
At Doune Castle, you can buy a coconut from the gift shop and bang the halves together as you walk the ramparts. If you don’t understand this joke yet, watch Monty Python before the trip. It’s required viewing.
Did you hear about the Wallace Monument’s fan club? Membership keeps growing — it’s monumental!
The Trossachs are sometimes called "the Highlands in miniature." The Highlands heard and they’re not taking it well.

Highlands & Loch Ness

What does Nessie eat for breakfast? Scotch eggs!
Which mythical creature never cleans its room? The Loch Mess Monster!
What did the Loch Ness Monster say to his long-lost friend? Long time no sea!
How can you communicate with the Loch Ness Monster? Drop him a line!
What time is it when Nessie jumps in your boat? Time to get a new boat.
What do you call a lazy Nessie? Loch Ness Mon-slacker.
I’m not saying I saw Nessie… but my story is lake-solid.
Why do Highlanders make great philosophers? They’re always contemplating the moor!
A tourist at Loch Ness asks a local: "Has the monster been seen this year?" The local replies: "Aye, but not since they raised the price of admission."
Glencoe is where the Massacre of 1692 happened. The MacDonalds were murdered by the Campbells who’d been their guests for two weeks. To this day, the Clachaig Inn in Glencoe has a sign: "No Campbells." They say it’s a joke. The MacDonalds aren’t laughing.
Culloden was the last pitched battle on British soil, in 1746. It lasted about an hour. The queue for the visitor centre gift shop now lasts longer.

Glasgow & Loch Lomond

A Glasgow woman goes to the dentist and settles down in the chair. "Comfy?" asks the dentist. "Govan," she replies. (Wordplay on Glasgow neighbourhood names — Comfy/Govan)
Glasgow was voted Europe’s murder capital AND the UK’s friendliest city. In the same week. We got our act together pronto. — Kevin Bridges
In Glasgow, "how" means "why." You don’t ponder why. You demand HOW? — Kevin Bridges
In Scotland, we call a dog a dug. We take that O and make it a U. You get dogs, then you get dugs. You get "Aww, look at that wee dog!" Then you get "Watch that dug!" — Kevin Bridges
If Kanye was born in Glasgow he would have been called No You Cannae. — Frankie Boyle
How do you know you’re staying at a hotel in Glasgow? When you call the front desk and say "I’ve got a leak in my sink" they tell you "Go ahead."
A tourist fishing for two weeks on Loch Lomond without a single bite finally hooked a salmon on the last day. "This fish has cost me six hundred dollars," he said. His guide replied: "Well sir, it’s a good thing you only caught the one."
They say you’ll take the high road and I’ll take the low road. In Glasgow, the low road is just the subway.
