Jokes by Location

General Scotland

Why did the Highlander go to the kilt shop? He heard they were having a hem-demic sale!
Have you heard about the new Scottish dating app? It’s called "Whisky Me Away."
What’s Scottish sunshine? A rumour!
I came, I saw, I kilt.
Why did the haggis get promoted? It had guts!
What do you call a Scot who’s run out of whisky? A tragedy!
40 degrees — Californians shiver uncontrollably. People in Scotland sunbathe.
What is the difference between a Scottish sheep farmer and a Rolling Stones song? One says "Hey you, get off of my cloud!" and the other says "Hey McCloud, get off of my ewe!"
Rain is just sky whisky!

Edinburgh

Enjoying a reign-y day here at Edinburgh Castle. Joust do it.
Why don’t secrets stay hidden in Edinburgh Castle? Too many tour guides!
What did the ghost of Greyfriars Bobby say? "This graveyard is ruff around the edges, but I love it!"
How many spirits haunt Greyfriars? Just the ones who checked in and never checked out!
I couldn’t have afforded to live in a castle, but it would have been peasant.

Stirling

Why did William Wallace bring a ladder to battle? He wanted to reach new heights!
What’s the difference between Stirling Castle and Mel Gibson? One actually looks like a legend!
Did you hear about the Wallace Monument’s fan club? Membership keeps growing — it’s monumental!

Highlands & Loch Ness

What does Nessie eat for breakfast? Scotch eggs!
Which mythical creature never cleans its room? The Loch Mess Monster!
What did the Loch Ness Monster say to his long-lost friend? Long time no sea!
How can you communicate with the Loch Ness Monster? Drop him a line!
Why do Highlanders make great philosophers? They’re always contemplating the moor!

Glasgow & Loch Lomond

A Glasgow woman goes to the dentist and settles down in the chair. "Comfy?" asks the dentist. "Govan," she replies. (Wordplay on Glasgow neighbourhood names — Comfy/Govan)
A tourist fishing for two weeks on Loch Lomond without getting a bite finally hooked a salmon on the last day. "This fish has cost me six hundred dollars," he said. His guide replied, "Well, Sir, it’s a good thing you only caught the one."
In Glasgow, "how" means "why." You don’t ponder why. You demand HOW?

Giant’s Causeway

Having a rock-solid time at the Giant’s Causeway — no pun intended!
Why did the giant use the causeway instead of flying? Because he wanted to keep his feet on the ground!
What’s the giant’s favourite type of music? Stone rock!

Belfast

What did the Titanic captain say when he saw a pile of cereal in the ocean? "Oh no, we’re going to hit an Ice-berg!"
Built in Belfast, buried in memes. (Belfast’s dark joke: "She was fine when she left here.")

Dublin

Why did the student bring a ladder to Trinity College? To reach higher learning!
I tried taking a quiet walk in Temple Bar — now I’m part of a street performance!
What did Molly Malone say to the statue? "You’re living my life — literally!"
The Spire is Dublin’s way of pointing out to visitors: "And that’s your way back to the hotel!"
Phoenix Park is so big, it takes longer to cross it than it takes to read the Book of Kells!
What do you call a deer in Phoenix Park that speaks Irish? A Gaelic stag!
How can you tell an Irishman is having a good time? He’s Dublin over with laughter!

Galway

Why do people love Galway so much? Because it’s the craic of the west!
The Spanish Arch in Galway isn’t Spanish — kind of like how Irish coffee isn’t coffee!
Galway is so charming, even the cobblestones have a sense of humour.

Inishmore & Aran Islands

Why did the tourist bring a boat to Inishmore? Because the WiFi doesn’t reach there either!
The Aran Islands: where time moves slower and the pints are stronger.
What do Aran Islanders call a quick trip? "Just a ferry away!"

Cliffs of Moher

"Moher" power to ya!
Leaving you with this cliff hanger!
The best place on Earth — Moher or less!
Can someone please take me back to the Cliffs of Insanity? I mean... Moher! (Princess Bride reference!)

Ring of Kerry & Killarney

Why is the Ring of Kerry called a "ring"? Because it keeps tourists going in circles!
What do you call the funny sound a tour bus makes on the Ring of Kerry? Sneem-thing amusing! (Sneem is a village on the route!)

Blarney Castle

A group of tourists visited Blarney Castle, and one asked, "Will kissing the stone make me Irish?" The guide said, "No, but the whiskey will!"
What did the tourist say after kissing the Blarney Stone? "Well, I haven’t gained the gift of the gab — I’m still just as quiet as ever!"

Irish Dance & Gaelic Games

I wasn’t kicking him. I was Riverdancing and he got in the way!
I saw an Irish dancing show called "Streamdance" — it’s like Riverdance, but it’s only a tributary act!
You know you’re an Irish dancer when your wedding dress costs less than your solo dress!
Hurling: where the ball gets hurt and the players are tougher!

General Ireland

It only rains twice a week in Ireland — once for three days, once for four!
What’s Irish and sits outside all day? Patty O’Furniture!
What do you call an Irishman who bounces off walls? Rick O’Shea!
An Irishman walks into a bar with a roll of asphalt under his arm and says, "Pint of Guinness please — and one for the road!"
Why should you never iron a four-leaf clover? You don’t want to press your luck!
An Irishman walks into a bar and orders three pints of Guinness. He takes a sip from each one in turn. When asked why, he explains: "I have two brothers — one in Australia, one in America. Every Saturday night, we drink together. So I’m having their drinks too!"
An English lawyer asks an Irishman why Irishmen reply to questions with another question. The Irishman says, "Who told you that?"
