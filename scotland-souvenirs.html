Souvenirs Worth Buying

Tartan Scarves & Blankets

Iconic Scottish textile. Pick one in a clan pattern or a general tartan. Look for authentic wool, not polyester tourist-shop knockoffs.

Harris Tweed

Authentic hand-woven fabric from the Outer Hebrides. Available as scarves, bags, and accessories. It's the real deal.

Shortbread Tins

Beautiful decorative tins filled with buttery shortbread. Perfect gifts for family back home.

Heathergems Jewelry

Unique jewelry made from real heather plant stems set in sterling silver. Brooches, earrings, and pendants. Only made in Scotland.

Highland Cow Plushies

Adorable stuffed versions of Scotland's fluffy ginger cattle. Impossible to resist.

The Sweet Haul

Tunnock's Tea Cakes & Caramel Wafers, Walkers Shortbread, Edinburgh Rock, and Scottish Tablet from Scotland. Kimberley Biscuits and Tayto from Ireland. Your suitcase = a portable candy shop.
