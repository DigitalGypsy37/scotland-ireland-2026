Movies & TV Shows

Watch before the trip to learn the history and culture of each location!

General Scotland (Multi-Location)

Outlander (TV, 2014–Present)

The ultimate Scotland prep. Covers Jacobite history across Edinburgh, Stirling (Doune Castle doubles as Castle Leoch), Glasgow, and the Highlands. Historical fantasy/romance with gorgeous production values. Great for all ages.

Braveheart (1995)

Mel Gibson as William Wallace. Stirling Bridge, Highland rebellion, Scottish independence. Historically loose but emotionally powerful. Some battle violence.

Outlaw King (2018)

Chris Pine as Robert the Bruce. Historians consider it more accurate than Braveheart. Netflix. Filmed across multiple Scottish locations.

Rob Roy (1995)

Liam Neeson as the Highland outlaw Robert Roy MacGregor. Filmed in Glencoe and Loch Lomond area. Action/historical.

Highlander (1986)

Filmed in Glencoe, Isle of Skye, Eilean Donan Castle, and Rannoch Moor. Action/fantasy about an immortal Scottish warrior.

Local Hero (1983)

Beloved Scottish classic about an American oil company buying a Highland village. Burt Lancaster. Mark Knopfler soundtrack. Charming for all ages.

Edinburgh

Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

Battle sequences filmed on the Royal Mile, St Giles’ Cathedral rooftop, and Waverley Station. Superhero action.

One Day (2024)

Netflix romance centered on Edinburgh University, featuring Old College and Grassmarket.

Fast & Furious 9 (2021)

Car chases filmed on George Street, Cockburn Street, Victoria Street, and the Royal Mile.

Trainspotting (1996)

Iconic Edinburgh film (though only 3 minutes were actually filmed there). Gritty. Adults only.

The Da Vinci Code (2006)

Scenes at Rosslyn Chapel, 8 miles from Edinburgh. Mystery/thriller.

A Castle for Christmas (2021)

Netflix holiday film using Edinburgh locations. Family/romance.

Harry Potter — Edinburgh Connection

J.K. Rowling wrote much of the series at the Elephant House Café, looking out at Greyfriars Kirkyard and Edinburgh Castle (the inspiration for Hogwarts).

Stirling & Trossachs

Braveheart (1995)

The real Battle of Stirling Bridge was fought here. Visit Stirling Castle and the Wallace Monument for context.

Outlander (TV)

Doune Castle (near Stirling) doubles as Castle Leoch. Culross village nearby also features heavily.

Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)

Multiple scenes filmed at Doune Castle—the same castle used for Outlander. Comedy classic.

Rob Roy (1953, Disney)

Earlier adaptation filmed in hills above Loch Ard near the Trossachs. Family adventure.

Highlands & Loch Ness & Glencoe & Culloden

Skyfall (2012)

James Bond returns to his Scottish roots. Extensive filming in Glencoe and Glen Etive. The iconic Bond/M/Aston Martin shot is at Buachaille Etive Mòr.

Harry Potter Films

Glenfinnan Viaduct (Hogwarts Express), Hagrid’s Hut at Clachaig Gully in Glencoe, Steall Falls (Quidditch), Glen Etive. Magic for all ages.

Brave (2012, Pixar)

Filmmakers visited Urquhart Castle on Loch Ness during pre-production, inspiring the film’s Highland setting. Animated family film.

Culloden (1964, BBC)

Essential viewing. Depicts the final Jacobite battle in documentary newsreel style. Filmed on Culloden Moor. Powerful historical context.

Highlander (1986)

Opening battle at Buachaille Etive Beag in Glencoe.

Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)

"Bridge of Death" scene filmed at the Meeting of Three Waters in Glencoe.

Glasgow & Loch Lomond

Outlander (TV)

George Square, Glasgow Cathedral, and Pollok Country Park feature prominently.

Vigil (2021, BBC)

Thriller set on a submarine off Scotland’s west coast, filmed at multiple Glasgow locations.

Succession (2021–2023)

Glasgow scenes double for London, including Blythswood Hotel.

General Ireland (Multi-Location)

The Wind That Shakes the Barley (2006)

Cillian Murphy. Palme d’Or winner about two brothers during the Irish War of Independence and Civil War. Cork. Essential Irish history.

Michael Collins (1996)

Liam Neeson as the revolutionary. Easter Rising through Civil War. Filmed in Dublin and Cork. Historical epic.

In the Name of the Father (1993)

Daniel Day-Lewis. Wrongful conviction during the Troubles. Gripping legal drama.

Brooklyn (2015)

Saoirse Ronan. Irish emigrant in 1950s America. Oscar-nominated. Beautiful and accessible.

The Commitments (1991)

Dublin band tries to play soul music. Hilarious. Captures working-class Dublin perfectly.

Once (2007)

Musical romance between a Dublin busker and Czech immigrant on Grafton Street. Won the Oscar for best song. Charming.

Sing Street (2016)

1980s Dublin teenager forms a band. Uplifting musical comedy. Great for teens.

Waking Ned Devine (1998)

Comedy about a small Irish village and a lottery win. Fun for the whole family.

The Secret of Roan Inish (1994)

Magical realist film blending Irish mythology and coastal life. Family-friendly.

Into the West (1992)

Family drama combining Irish culture with magical realism and Traveller culture.

Angela’s Ashes (1999)

Frank McCourt’s memoir. 1930s–40s Irish poverty. Cork, Limerick, Dublin.

The Irish Revolution (2017, BBC/RTÉ)

Three-part documentary covering 1916–1923. Essential 20th-century Irish history context.

Giant’s Causeway

Game of Thrones (TV, 2011–2019)

Much of the series was filmed throughout Northern Ireland: Titanic Studios (interiors), The Dark Hedges, Cushendun Caves, Castle Ward (Winterfell), Ballintoy Harbour. The Causeway Coast is GoT country.

Dungeons & Dragons: Honour Among Thieves (2023)

Fantasy adventure showcasing the Causeway Coast.

Belfast

Belfast (2021)

Kenneth Branagh’s black-and-white film about a boy’s family during the outbreak of the Troubles (1969). Personal and moving.

’71 (2014)

Young British soldier caught behind enemy lines in 1971 Belfast. Tense thriller.

Good Vibrations (2012)

Terri Hooley’s punk record shop during the Troubles. Music and cultural resilience.

Mickybo and Me (2004)

Protestant and Catholic boys’ friendship in 1970 Belfast. Heartwarming.

Game of Thrones (TV)

Interior scenes filmed at Titanic Studios in Belfast.

Dublin

Once (2007)

Set on Grafton Street and throughout Dublin. Musical romance. Oscar winner.

Sing Street (2016)

1980s Dublin. Teen band. Uplifting.

The Commitments (1991)

Working-class Dublin soul band. Hilarious.

My Left Foot (1989)

Daniel Day-Lewis as Dubliner Christy Brown. Oscar winner.

The Dead (1987)

John Huston’s adaptation of James Joyce’s story. Dublin literary culture.

Michael Collins (1996)

Dublin independence landmarks. Historical epic.

Leap Year (2010)

Romantic comedy. Partly Dublin, partly Galway/Connemara.

Galway

The Quiet Man (1952)

John Ford classic filmed in Cong (Galway/Mayo border). Irish-American returns to his birthplace.

The Guard (2011)

Brendan Gleeson and Don Cheadle. Comedy-thriller set in coastal Galway. Hilarious.

Calm with Horses (2019)

Crime drama, ~80% filmed in Galway.

Leap Year (2010)

Scenes in Galway and Connemara.

Inishmore & Aran Islands

The Banshees of Inisherin (2022)

Oscar-nominated. Colin Farrell and Brendan Gleeson. Filmed ON Inishmore, featuring Dún Aonghasa and the island’s coastline. A must-watch before visiting.

Man of Aran (1934)

Pioneering documentary about Aran Island life and the sea. Historical.

Cliffs of Moher

The Princess Bride (1987)

The Cliffs appear as the "Cliffs of Insanity." Westley scales them to rescue Buttercup. Classic for all ages.

Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince (2009)

Harry and Dumbledore’s cave scene was filmed here. The only HP scene shot outside the UK.

Ring of Kerry & Killarney

Star Wars: The Force Awakens (2015) & The Last Jedi (2017)

Filmed on Skellig Michael (off the Iveragh Peninsula). Rey finds Luke Skywalker. Also scenes on the Dingle Peninsula.

Far and Away (1992)

Tom Cruise and Nicole Kidman. Famine-era drama filmed in Dunquin on the Dingle Peninsula.

Ryan’s Daughter (1970)

David Lean epic with spectacular Dingle scenery. Won Oscars for cinematography.

The Lobster (2015)

Surreal dark comedy partly set at Parknasilla Resort near Sneem on the Ring of Kerry.

Excalibur (1981)

Arthurian legend filmed in Kerry. Liam Neeson, Patrick Stewart, Helen Mirren.

Blarney Castle & Cork Area

The Wind That Shakes the Barley (2006)

Set in Cork during the War of Independence. Cillian Murphy.

Angela’s Ashes (1999)

Partly filmed in Cork.

Irish Dance & Gaelic Games

Jig (2011)

Documentary about the World Irish Dance Championships. First behind-the-scenes look at competitive Irish dance.

Irish Dance: Steps of Freedom (PBS Documentary)

Traces 2,000 years of Irish dance history.

The Game: The Story of Hurling (2018–2019, TV)

Documentary celebrating Ireland’s national sport.

Pre-Trip Viewing Plan

Weeks 4–3 before the trip (General context):
  1. Outlander (TV) — Broad Scottish history and locations overview
  2. The Irish Revolution (2017 documentary) — Essential Irish history
  3. Michael Collins (1996) — Irish independence
Weeks 2–1 before the trip (Location-specific):
  1. Harry Potter films — Highland/Glencoe scenery
  2. Braveheart or Outlaw King — Stirling/Scottish independence
  3. The Banshees of Inisherin — Inishmore
  4. Belfast (2021) — Belfast and the Troubles
  5. Once or Sing Street — Dublin atmosphere
Last few days:
  1. The Princess Bride — Cliffs of Moher
  2. Skyfall — Glencoe beauty

By Audience

Teens & Young Adults: Harry Potter, Game of Thrones, Sing Street, The Banshees of Inisherin, Brave, Star Wars
Adults: Outlander, Belfast, The Wind That Shakes the Barley, Once, The Guard
Grandparents: The Quiet Man, Local Hero, My Left Foot, Ryan’s Daughter
Whole Family: The Princess Bride, Brave, Waking Ned Devine, Once, Outlander
