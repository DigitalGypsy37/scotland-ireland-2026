Souvenirs Worth Buying

Claddagh Ring

Two hands holding a heart topped with a crown: friendship, love, and loyalty. Originated in the Claddagh village in Galway — the perfect place to buy one.

Aran Sweater

Traditional wool sweaters with intricate cable-knit patterns, each with a specific meaning. Best purchased on the Aran Islands themselves or in Galway.

Connemara Marble

Beautiful green marble unique to western Ireland. Available as jewelry, ornaments, and home goods.

Barry's Tea & Tayto Crisps

The taste of Ireland in a bag. Cheap, lightweight, and everyone back home will love them.

The Sweet Haul

Tunnock's Tea Cakes & Caramel Wafers, Walkers Shortbread, Edinburgh Rock, and Scottish Tablet from Scotland. Kimberley Biscuits and Tayto from Ireland. Your suitcase = a portable candy shop.
