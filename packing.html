Packing List

July in Scotland and Ireland means mild temps (55–66°F / 12–19°C) but unpredictable weather. You can easily get four seasons in a single day, so layers are everything.

Clothing Essentials

  • Lightweight waterproof rain jacket (you WILL need this)
  • 2–3 long-sleeve shirts for layering
  • T-shirts and breathable tops
  • 1–2 cardigans or light sweaters
  • Jeans or pants (2–3 pairs)
  • Shorts (for warmer days — yes, they happen!)
  • One nicer outfit for farewell/welcome dinners
  • Swimwear (for Kilmurvey Beach on Inishmore)
  • Warm hat or beanie for boat rides and Highlands
  • Lightweight scarf
  • Sunglasses

Footwear

  • Comfortable waterproof walking shoes (you'll walk A LOT)
  • Sturdy hiking boots (for Dún Aonghasa, Ring of Kerry, Highlands)
  • Casual shoes or sandals for evenings

Gear & Accessories

  • UK/Ireland power adapter (Type G — 3 rectangular prongs). Same adapter works in both countries!
  • Portable phone charger / power bank
  • Small umbrella
  • Day pack or small backpack
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Midge/bug repellent (essential for the Scottish Highlands — tiny biting insects thrive in July)
  • Sunscreen & lip balm with SPF
  • Moisturizer (wind + rain = dry skin)

Documents & Money

  • Valid passport
  • Travel insurance info
  • Credit/debit cards (Visa & Mastercard widely accepted)
  • Some British Pounds (£) for Scotland & Northern Ireland
  • Some Euros (€) for Republic of Ireland
  • Prescriptions & medications
Currency heads-up: You'll use TWO currencies on this trip. Scotland and Belfast use British Pounds (£). Once you cross into the Republic of Ireland (Dublin onward), everything switches to Euros (€). They are NOT interchangeable. Cards work almost everywhere, but it's smart to carry some cash in each currency.
