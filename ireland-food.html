Irish Stew
Hearty lamb, root vegetables, and potatoes slow-cooked into a warm hug in a bowl. The definitive comfort food.
Boxty
Traditional Irish potato pancakes, served with everything from smoked salmon to stew. Try them at Gallagher's Boxty House in Temple Bar, Dublin.
Soda Bread
Dense, slightly sweet buttermilk bread with golden raisins. No yeast — just baking soda. Served warm with Irish butter, it's heavenly.
Galway Oysters
World-famous oysters from Clarinbridge, County Galway. Best enjoyed fresh with a squeeze of lemon (or paired with a pint of Guinness for the adventurous).
Seafood Chowder
Creamy, chunky, and loaded with fresh local seafood. Especially incredible along the west coast in Galway.
Beef & Guinness Pie
Slow-cooked beef in a rich Guinness sauce under a golden pastry crust. Pub food perfection.
Full Irish Breakfast
Eggs, bacon, sausage, black & white pudding, beans, toast, grilled tomato. The fuel you need for a full day of adventuring.

Irish Sweets & Treats

Murphy's Ice Cream

Unique Irish flavors like brown bread, sea salt, and Irish coffee. Shops in Dublin and Killarney.

Tayto Crisps

Ireland's beloved potato chips. Cheese & Onion flavor is iconic. Grab a bag (or ten).

Barry's Tea

Ireland runs on tea, and Barry's is the gold standard. Pick up a box to bring the taste of Ireland home.

Queen of Tarts

A Dublin bakery institution for 25+ years. Everything baked on-site with local ingredients. Don't skip dessert here.

Pub culture tip: Irish pubs aren't just for drinks — they're communal living rooms. Look for pubs with live traditional music (called "trad sessions"). Musicians often play together spontaneously. The Cobblestone in Dublin has sessions 7 nights a week. In Galway, try Tigh Coili's. Order food, sit back, and soak in the atmosphere.
