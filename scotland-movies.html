Movies & TV Shows

Watch before the trip to learn the history and culture of each location!

General Scotland (Multi-Location)

Outlander (TV, 2014–Present)

The ultimate Scotland prep. Covers Jacobite history across Edinburgh, Stirling (Doune Castle doubles as Castle Leoch), Glasgow, and the Highlands. Historical fantasy/romance with gorgeous production values. Great for all ages.

Braveheart (1995)

Mel Gibson as William Wallace. Stirling Bridge, Highland rebellion, Scottish independence. Historically loose but emotionally powerful. Some battle violence.

Outlaw King (2018)

Chris Pine as Robert the Bruce. Historians consider it more accurate than Braveheart. Netflix. Filmed across multiple Scottish locations.

Rob Roy (1995)

Liam Neeson as the Highland outlaw Robert Roy MacGregor. Filmed in Glencoe and Loch Lomond area. Action/historical.

Highlander (1986)

Filmed in Glencoe, Isle of Skye, Eilean Donan Castle, and Rannoch Moor. Action/fantasy about an immortal Scottish warrior.

Local Hero (1983)

Beloved Scottish classic about an American oil company buying a Highland village. Burt Lancaster. Mark Knopfler soundtrack. Charming for all ages.

Edinburgh

Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

Battle sequences filmed on the Royal Mile, St Giles’ Cathedral rooftop, and Waverley Station. Superhero action.

One Day (2024)

Netflix romance centered on Edinburgh University, featuring Old College and Grassmarket.

Fast & Furious 9 (2021)

Car chases filmed on George Street, Cockburn Street, Victoria Street, and the Royal Mile.

Trainspotting (1996)

Iconic Edinburgh film (though only 3 minutes were actually filmed there). Gritty. Adults only.

The Da Vinci Code (2006)

Scenes at Rosslyn Chapel, 8 miles from Edinburgh. Mystery/thriller.

A Castle for Christmas (2021)

Netflix holiday film using Edinburgh locations. Family/romance.

Harry Potter — Edinburgh Connection

J.K. Rowling wrote much of the series at the Elephant House Café, looking out at Greyfriars Kirkyard and Edinburgh Castle (the inspiration for Hogwarts).

Stirling & Trossachs

Braveheart (1995)

The real Battle of Stirling Bridge was fought here. Visit Stirling Castle and the Wallace Monument for context.

Outlander (TV)

Doune Castle (near Stirling) doubles as Castle Leoch. Culross village nearby also features heavily.

Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)

Multiple scenes filmed at Doune Castle—the same castle used for Outlander. Comedy classic.

Rob Roy (1953, Disney)

Earlier adaptation filmed in hills above Loch Ard near the Trossachs. Family adventure.

Highlands & Loch Ness & Glencoe & Culloden

Skyfall (2012)

James Bond returns to his Scottish roots. Extensive filming in Glencoe and Glen Etive. The iconic Bond/M/Aston Martin shot is at Buachaille Etive Mòr.

Harry Potter Films

Glenfinnan Viaduct (Hogwarts Express), Hagrid’s Hut at Clachaig Gully in Glencoe, Steall Falls (Quidditch), Glen Etive. Magic for all ages.

Brave (2012, Pixar)

Filmmakers visited Urquhart Castle on Loch Ness during pre-production, inspiring the film’s Highland setting. Animated family film.

Culloden (1964, BBC)

Essential viewing. Depicts the final Jacobite battle in documentary newsreel style. Filmed on Culloden Moor. Powerful historical context.

Highlander (1986)

Opening battle at Buachaille Etive Beag in Glencoe.

Monty Python and the Holy Grail (1975)

"Bridge of Death" scene filmed at the Meeting of Three Waters in Glencoe.

Glasgow & Loch Lomond

Outlander (TV)

George Square, Glasgow Cathedral, and Pollok Country Park feature prominently.

Vigil (2021, BBC)

Thriller set on a submarine off Scotland’s west coast, filmed at multiple Glasgow locations.

Succession (2021–2023)

Glasgow scenes double for London, including Blythswood Hotel.
