The Wind That Shakes the Barley (2006)

Cillian Murphy. Palme d’Or winner about two brothers during the Irish War of Independence and Civil War. Cork. Essential Irish history.

Michael Collins (1996)

Liam Neeson as the revolutionary. Easter Rising through Civil War. Filmed in Dublin and Cork. Historical epic.

In the Name of the Father (1993)

Daniel Day-Lewis. Wrongful conviction during the Troubles. Gripping legal drama.

Brooklyn (2015)

Saoirse Ronan. Irish emigrant in 1950s America. Oscar-nominated. Beautiful and accessible.

The Commitments (1991)

Dublin band tries to play soul music. Hilarious. Captures working-class Dublin perfectly.

Once (2007)

Musical romance between a Dublin busker and Czech immigrant on Grafton Street. Won the Oscar for best song. Charming.

Sing Street (2016)

1980s Dublin teenager forms a band. Uplifting musical comedy. Great for teens.

Waking Ned Devine (1998)

Comedy about a small Irish village and a lottery win. Fun for the whole family.

The Secret of Roan Inish (1994)

Magical realist film blending Irish mythology and coastal life. Family-friendly.

Into the West (1992)

Family drama combining Irish culture with magical realism and Traveller culture.

Angela’s Ashes (1999)

Frank McCourt’s memoir. 1930s–40s Irish poverty. Cork, Limerick, Dublin.

The Irish Revolution (2017, BBC/RTÉ)

Three-part documentary covering 1916–1923. Essential 20th-century Irish history context.
